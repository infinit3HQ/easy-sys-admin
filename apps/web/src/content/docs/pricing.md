Three ways to run Easy Sys Admin. The public beta is what we host. Self-Hosted is the same software, run by you. Managed Cloud is not available yet. Nothing on this page takes a payment. The same cards are on the pricing page.

<!-- plans -->

Free Beta is the hosted service at [easysysadmin.app](https://easysysadmin.app). Each space can hold 10 servers, 20 in total, with one personal space and one organization. Sign in with Google or email.

Self-Hosted is the open-source release under AGPL-3.0-only. It is free for any use, including commercial use. The license sets no usage cap. The default is 500 servers per account, and you can change that setting. The [self-hosting guide](/self-hosting) is the install.

Managed Cloud is the service we host, update, back up, and monitor, with priority support. The price is not set. Joining the waitlist sends mail to support@easysysadmin.app. It does not start a subscription.
