Three ways to run Easy Sys Admin. The public beta is free and is the plan in force today. Self-Hosted and Managed Cloud are not available yet. Nothing on this page takes a payment. The same cards are in the web app.

<PricingTable />

Free Beta is the hosted service at [easysysadmin.app](https://easysysadmin.app). Each space can hold 10 servers, 20 in total, with one personal space and one organization. Sign in with Google or email.

Self-Hosted is the open-source release under AGPL-3.0-only. It is free for any use, including commercial use. Unlimited servers is the aim of that release. It is coming soon. The [self-hosting guide](/self-hosting) describes what the source tree can do today, including the current default caps.

Managed Cloud is the service we host, update, back up, and monitor, with priority support. The price is not set. Joining the waitlist sends mail to support@easysysadmin.app. It does not start a subscription.
